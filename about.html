Our story

Botanical extracts from the Qinling foothills, held to one standard: quality you can verify

Based in Xi'an, Shaanxi — the heartland of China's medicinal plant resources — our company exports high-quality medicinal-edible (traditional herbal) and health & wellness botanical extracts to global markets.

We simplify the complexities of traditional trading to supply direct, high-purity natural ingredients, with uncompromised quality and operational flexibility.

Why Choose Us

Sourced from the Hub of Extraction: Based in Xi'an, we partner long-term with top-tier, rigorously audited local source manufacturers, ensuring stable pricing and strict batch-to-batch consistency from farm to finished powder.

Data-Driven Quality Control: Quality is non-negotiable. Every batch undergoes strict testing and HPLC (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography) analysis before release, delivered with a full, transparent COA (Certificate of Analysis) and chromatogram.

Low MOQ & Flexible Support: Supporting 1 kg minimum orders to lower your trial and R&D costs. We also provide professional OEM/ODM services, including private labeling, custom packaging, and compliance documentation support.

Agile, 1-on-1 Communication: No bureaucratic delays. Enjoy rapid responses, tailored sample arrangements, and seamless door-to-door logistics tailored to your project timeline.

If you are looking for a reliable, transparent partner for your next health and wellness formulation, contact us today to discuss your requirements or request a quote.

Why Xi'an

Built where the botanicals grow

Xi'an sits at the northern foot of the Qinling mountains — the range the Chinese have called “the natural pharmacy” for two thousand years. It is one of China's richest botanical growing regions and the centre of its plant-extract industry.

Working here is our advantage, and yours. Raw materials arrive within hours of the growing areas, not weeks. The audited production facilities and QC laboratories we cooperate with are all within a day's drive, which means every specification we quote can be checked at the source. And we keep the range deliberately narrow — food & medicine homology botanicals — so that when your QA team asks hard questions, we answer from the current production lot, not from a catalog page.

  • Direct from the source — raw materials come from the Qinling growing region, qualified before they enter production
  • Strict quality control — written specifications, HPLC release testing and full batch documentation on every lot
  • OEM & private label — your brand, your specification, your label — from 1 kg
  • One accountable contact — the person who quotes your price controls your quality
