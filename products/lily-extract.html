Lily Bulb Extract

Lily bulb extract standardised on lily polysaccharides — the traditional calming botanical of Chinese sleep formulas, modernised for gummies, capsules and soothing evening drinks.

  • Lily polysaccharides (per lot COA)
  • White to pale yellow fine powder
  • Traditional calming botanical
  • Mild, pleasant taste
