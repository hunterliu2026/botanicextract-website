Five documented benefits

01

Brain development “gold”

The brain holds the body's highest sialic-acid concentration; it supports cognitive development, especially in infants and children.

02

Better nutrient absorption

Improves intestinal absorption of nutrients — a natural carrier effect documented in infant nutrition.

03

Cell recognition & defense

Key role in cell-membrane recognition and neutralization of toxins, including protection against pathogenic E. coli.

04

Immunity support

Especially valuable in children's nutrition; supports the body's defense system.

05

Cell protection & longevity

Protects and stabilizes cell structures — the basis of its beauty-from-within and premium-tonic applications.

Questions buyers ask about this product

Which grade should I choose?

Food-and-beverage formulas commonly use the 20% food grade; premium nootropic and tonic brands use the 98% purified grade. Tell us your label claim and target market and we will match the grade within one email.

Is it the same as bird's nest sialic acid?

Yes — the molecule is identical (N-acetylneuraminic acid). Our material is produced by fermentation/extraction at pharmaceutical standard, giving a consistent, animal-free supply at a fraction of the cost of bird's nest.

Can you pack under our own brand?

Yes. We ship in your branded drums or bags and apply your labels.
