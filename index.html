Who we are

Your botanical extract supplier at the foot of the Qinling mountains

BotanicExtract is Hunter Liu's supply practice, built in Xi'an — the city at the northern foot of the Qinling mountains, the range the Chinese have called “the natural pharmacy” for two thousand years. Working where the raw botanicals grow means shorter lines from herb to drum, direct relationships with growers and audited production facilities, and one quality gate we control personally: every lot is released against a written specification, HPLC-tested and documented before it ships.

How to ensure product quality

Four steps between a botanical and your drum

1

Specification locked first

Before anything is sourced, we agree a written specification: assay, test method, mesh size, limits. No surprises later.

2

Audited manufacturing partners

Your material is produced by GMP-audited extraction facilities we work with under written agreement — we inspect, we do not guess.

3

Testing before release

A batch COA covering HPLC assay, heavy metals and microbiology ships with every lot — drawn from the same batch as your goods, not from a catalogue.

4

Documents released with the batch

The batch COA — electronic copy as standard, paper copy available on request — and the packing list ship together with the goods, with the test methods (HPLC/TLC, heavy metals) stated on the report.

Lead time

From confirmed order to dispatch, step by step

So you know exactly which stage you are waiting on — and can plan your launch around it.

  • In-stock items — ready in about 2 days
  • Bulk production — 3–5 working days after deposit for standard grades
  • QC release & packing — 1–2 working days; the batch COA is issued at this stage
  • Inland transport — Xi'an to our Shenzhen consolidation warehouse, about 3 days
  • Air freight — about 6 days to major U.S. and EU airports (sea freight 18–25 days)
  • Door to door by air — typically about 11 days

We build a buffer into the schedule for testing and documentation, and confirm the working dates in writing on the PI — so the date you are given is a date we can hold.
